Recreates lockpicking minigame from Fallout 3/New Vegas, Fallout 4, Skyrim:

Lock shown head-on with bobby pin or lockpick (in our game, we will use standard vocab "lockpick") sticking out of the lock. Lockpick is basically a line shape protruding radially from the top of the keyhole. Moving the mouse left or right rotates the lockpick about the origin in the top of the keyhole; the lockpick has a 180 degree rotation range (the top half of the unit circle). Somewhere within the range, there is a "sweet spot" whose size varies based upon Lock Level and Security skill (standard terms). The player can press A and/or D to action the lock, rotating the whole thing. (This should correspondingly move the top of the keyhole, and consequently the translate the lockpick.) The closer the player is to the sweet spot, the further the lock can be actioned before jamming; when jamming, the lock and pick shake and damage is done to the lockpick. If the lockpick is in the sweet spot, the lock turns all the way 90 degrees clockwise and opens. The player is presented with a new lock and the gameplay loop continues.

Player has finite # of lockpicks. Lockpicks can be bought at the shop. Start with 5 lockpicks (arbitrary #). Lockpicks have finite HP; they break when jammed for too many time units.

At any point in the game, player can click a "Shop" button which presents a menu to buy lockpicks and level Security skill. When Security reaches 100, player can Prestige which resets Security to 20 and money to 0

Currency rewarded upon successful lockpick. Currency depends on Lock Level.

Lock Level is random within a range from 1-100.

Security level starts at 20, and can be upgraded all the way up to 100. As Security increases, locks get easier -- sweet spots get bigger. Where Security matches Lock Level, the sweet spot is decently big; where e.g. security is 80 points behind Lock Level, the sweet spot is very small only a few degrees.
